the maker

Candlesnart started with one dropped candle and one very good stain on the studio floor. We stared at it for a week, then framed the floor.

Every candle is poured in small batches, painted by hand with more enthusiasm than restraint, and named after the mood it was painted in.

No two are alike, because our hands shake slightly when we're excited, and we are always excited.

The paintings in the shop are made the honest way: we burn our own candles, let them collapse onto canvas, and sign whatever gravity decides.
